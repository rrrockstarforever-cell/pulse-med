Picture review: 4 questions with a picture

#1 im-derma-lesions

A 31-year-old woman has a larger scaly plaque on her trunk followed several days later by multiple smaller oval scaly plaques. A clinical photograph shows the smaller plaques distributed over her back. Which characteristic arrangement is expected for the smaller plaques?

Answer: D. Christmas-tree pattern

#3 im-path-histo

A biopsy section from a superior nasal-cavity mass in a 36-year-old woman shows lobules of uniform small round cells separated by delicate vascular stroma. The cells have scant cytoplasm and finely stippled chromatin, with focal fibrillary material between them Which tumour best fits these findings?

Answer: A. Olfactory neuroblastoma

#4 im-bone-xray

A 67-year-old man with renal cell carcinoma receiving sunitinib reports progressive pain in the proximal femur. An AP X-ray shows a metastatic lesion with both areas of bone destruction and areas of increased density. Which set of radiographic descriptors is used for the lesion component of the Mirels score?

Answer: B. Blastic, mixed, lytic
